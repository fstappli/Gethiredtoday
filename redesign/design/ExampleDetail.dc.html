How to write a Product Designer resume

1. Lead with outcomes in your summary

Name your speciality, years of experience and one result. Keep it to three or four lines.

EXAMPLE SUMMARY

Product designer with 8 years of experience shipping consumer and fintech apps. I turn research into measurable wins: higher conversion, faster flows and calmer support queues.

2. Quantify every bullet you can

Recruiters scan for numbers. Users, revenue, time saved and ratings all count.

  • Redesigned checkout for 2.1M monthly shoppers, lifting conversion by 14%.
  • Built a 120-component design system that cut build time by 30%.
  • Ran 40+ usability sessions that shaped a new onboarding flow.
