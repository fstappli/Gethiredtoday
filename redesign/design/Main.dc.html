Questions, answered

Is it really free to start?

Yes. Build, score and download one resume as a PDF without a card. Pro unlocks unlimited resumes, Word export, job matching and Auto-Apply.

What does Auto-Apply send in my name?

A version of your resume tailored to each job. In review mode nothing is sent until you approve it.

Can I import my existing resume?

Yes. Upload a PDF or Word file, or paste your LinkedIn profile link.

How is this different from a general AI chatbot?

It is built only for job search: recruiter-tested templates, a live ATS score, job matching and application tracking in one place.

How do I cancel?

Go to Plan & billing and choose Cancel. Two clicks, and your resumes stay in your account.
